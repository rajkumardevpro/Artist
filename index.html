The artist

Creating quietly.
Capturing deeply.

Nandini is a graphite and charcoal artist whose work explores realism, emotion, and quiet human presence. Each drawing is built slowly through observation, texture, shadow, and restraint.

Her practice seeks the feeling beneath the visible: a glance, an instinct, a pause, or a story that does not need words.
