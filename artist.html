The artist

Nandini
Patel.

Nandini is a graphite and charcoal artist from India. Her practice explores realism, emotion, and quiet human presence through detailed pencil marks, tonal contrast, and patient observation.

Creating quietly, capturing deeply—her work looks for the feeling beneath what is visible: a glance, an instinct, a pause, or a story that does not need words.

Where silence finds its form.

Every artwork is created by hand in graphite and charcoal on paper. Original works and custom commissions are available through direct enquiry.
